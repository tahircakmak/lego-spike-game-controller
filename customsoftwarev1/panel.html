Bindings

Variables

Numbers your bindings can change (var_add / var_set / var_reset actions) and react to (use var:name as a source or a condition). Min / max are limits; with wrap on, going past one end jumps to the other end (handy for modes).

Systems

A system is a group of bindings. Switch a whole system on or off (here, or with a "system" action), or give it conditions so it only works while they are true, for example var:mode at 1.

Hub

The wizard measures every detected device. Every number below can also be changed by hand: press Save calibration afterwards. Changing a motor's mode, rest position or spring sends the hub program again (about 2 seconds).
